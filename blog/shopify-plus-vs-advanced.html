Shopify Plus vs Advanced Shopify: Which Plan Does Your Business Need?

Detailed feature-by-feature comparison between Shopify Plus and Advanced Shopify. Understand transaction fee savings, checkout extensibility, and B2B wholesale ROI.

Shopify Plus ($2,300/mo) vs Advanced Shopify ($399/mo)

Deciding when to upgrade to Shopify Plus is one of the most critical financial and operational decisions for an e-commerce business. Here is the realistic breakdown of features, transaction fees, and breakeven calculations.

Feature Comparison Table

Feature Advanced Shopify ($399/mo) Shopify Plus ($2,300/mo)
Checkout Customization Standard theme only Full Checkout Extensibility & Custom Apps
B2B Wholesale Portal No (requires apps) Native wholesale pricing, catalogs & terms
Expansion Stores 1 store only Up to 9 expansion stores included
Shopify Flow Automation Basic triggers Enterprise automated workflows & actions
Transaction Fees 0.6% on 3rd-party gateways 0.20% on 3rd-party gateways
Launchpad Tool No Automated flash sales & catalog scheduling

When Does Shopify Plus Make Sense?

From a purely financial standpoint, if your store generates over $800,000 to $1,000,000 in annual gross merchandise value (GMV), the lower transaction fees (0.20% vs 0.6%) offset a large portion of the $2,300 monthly fee. Furthermore, if you need native B2B wholesale portals or custom checkout rules, Shopify Plus is essential.
